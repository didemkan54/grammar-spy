Grammar Spy - Simple Present Diagnostic

Directions:
1) Read each sentence.
2) Correct the verb form if needed.
3) Write one grammar reason.

1. She walk to class every day.
2. They is ready for the quiz.
3. My brother and I studies at night.
4. The teacher check homework on Fridays.
5. It rain a lot in spring.

Extension:
Rewrite one corrected sentence in a longer academic sentence.
