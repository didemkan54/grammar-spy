FAQ

Questions teachers ask first

Is this for middle school or high school?

Both. Missions are designed for middle and high school grammar routines, including mixed-level classes.

Do students need accounts?

No account is required for student play in classroom setups. Teachers can run missions and share access quickly.

How long does a mission take?

Most missions fit in about 10 to 15 minutes, so they work as warm-ups, stations, or homework practice.

Can I use it for ELD students?

Yes. Grammar Spy includes clear language cues and structure-focused practice that supports ELD learners.

Is it free?

You can start with a free mission and free diagnostic resources, then decide whether to expand your access.

How do I set up classes?

Create a teacher account, launch your first mission, and share class access in minutes.
